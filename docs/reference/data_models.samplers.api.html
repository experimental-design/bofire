Sampler

data_models.samplers.api.Sampler()

Method that spreads sample points over the inputs, e.g. for an initial design.

The sampler spreads points over the unit cube; every input maps its coordinate onto its values, categorical and discrete inputs by equally wide bins.

Samplers are immutable: their options can’t be changed after construction.

UniformSampler

data_models.samplers.api.UniformSampler()

Draws every input independently and uniformly at random.

Uniform sampling can lead to clustering of points. Consider a low-discrepancy sampler such as SobolSampler to cover the space more evenly.

Examples

>>> UniformSampler()

SobolSampler

data_models.samplers.api.SobolSampler()

Draws points of a Sobol sequence, a quasi-random design that covers the domain evenly.

The coverage is best when the number of points is a power of two.

Examples

>>> SobolSampler(scramble=True)

LatinHypercubeSampler

data_models.samplers.api.LatinHypercubeSampler()

Draws a Latin hypercube design: the range of every input is split into as many intervals as there are points, and every interval holds exactly one point.

Examples

>>> LatinHypercubeSampler(optimization="random-cd")

### Sampler { #bofire.data_models.samplers.api.Sampler }

```python
data_models.samplers.api.Sampler()
```

Method that spreads sample points over the inputs, e.g. for an initial design.

The sampler spreads points over the unit cube; every input maps its coordinate
onto its values, categorical and discrete inputs by equally wide bins.

Samplers are immutable: their options can't be changed after construction.
